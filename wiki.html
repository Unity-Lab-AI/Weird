Overview

You are the villain — the dungeon operator, the predator, the collector. DUNGEON MASTER: THE HUNT is a persistent text-and-emoji city-builder wearing the skin of a dungeon-harem evil-taboo simulator. Gather resources (money from film sales + captured girls), invest in construction (dungeon + per-hold upgrades), expand operations (more girls, harder grounds, bigger dungeons), and grow forever. There is no win state — the game is the ever-growing collection and the ever-upgrading dungeon.

Every girl is procedurally generated from an archetype, runs on a local uncensored LLM (Ollama) for live dialogue, gets her own TTS voice (Kokoro), and a seeded face + outfit (Pollinations) kept consistent across every image of her. Everything persists locally in your browser (IndexedDB) and nothing leaves your machine.

Adult fiction, 18+ only. Every template and procedural spawn is locked to an 18+ floor. Taboo content stays in fiction.

How to Play

  1. Set up your tools. Run Ollama locally (an uncensored model — dolphin-mistral / abliterated), and optionally drop a Pollinations key in Settings for images + load Kokoro for voice. See the landing ⚡ Setup.
  2. Start a new game. You begin with a basic dungeon and one starter captive already in a hold. Name the honorific your girls call you.
  3. Go hunting. Open Town, pick a hunting ground, buy capture tools from the shop, and run the 4-stage capture attempt.
  4. Bring her home. She's assigned a hold. Open her room to talk, feed/water her, drug her, dress her, and interact.
  5. Make money. Record films of your sessions and let them sell passively; whore her out to johns; sell milk; later sell body parts.
  6. Reinvest. Spend on dungeon + per-hold upgrades, more tools, more dungeons, owned properties, and consumables.
  7. Level your DEPRAVITY. Every act earns XP. Higher levels unlock the heaviest depravities.
  8. Manage the threats. Keep escape-risk down and WANTED heat low. Pause the game (⏸) any time — progress is saved and reloads when you return.
Sandbox / cheat start unlocks everything up front for a consequence-free playground (raids off, all tiers open). Normal mode makes you earn it.

The Core Loop

A self-reinforcing city-builder cycle. Every step ratchets the operation forward.

  1. 🎯 Hunt prey in Town with purchased tools.
  2. ⛓️ Hold her in an upgradeable dungeon hold.
  3. 💬 Interact / indoctrinate — care, drugs, sex, violence, toys; raise her Stockholm bond, mark her as yours.
  4. 🎬 Record + rent — films sell forever; johns pay per visit; milk + body parts add income.
  5. 😈 Level DEPRAVITY — unlock harder grounds + darker acts; or pay a fee to unlock early.
  6. 🏗️ Reinvest + expand — upgrades, tools, dungeons, properties.
  7. 🩸 Retire — trade, snuff, dispose; keep a keepsake trophy with a permanent buff.

Threaded through all of it: keep her from escaping, and keep your WANTED heat below the raid line.

Hunting & Capture

Town hosts the hunting grounds, each with its own spawn pool of girls + an exposure (witness) risk. Harder grounds host rarer, more vibrant girls and unlock with progression.

Hunting grounds

Main Street · Pink Room Club · Old Library · Westwood Park · Iron Gym · Downtown Mall · Cup & Saucer (coffee shop) · Greek Row (sorority) · Backroads (remote) · Grand Hotel Lobby · Private Party · Campus Quad — plus your neighborhood.

Gating: some grounds need a DEPRAVITY level, a minimum captives-held count, or bought private access — e.g. mall (2 captives), sorority (L3), hotel lobby (paid), private party (L3 + 4 captives), campus (L5), remote (L6). Owning a property unlocks no-suspicion private hunts there.

The 4-stage capture

Approach → Engage → Subdue → Secure. Each stage rolls per-tool stats against per-archetype resistance. Capture tools leave healable injury (a lead pipe wounds; chemicals sicken). A botched grab in the open or a witnessed one raises WANTED; repeated misses can let her escape.

Capture tools

Sedatives: Sedative Vial (rohypnol), Clear Solvent (chloroform), Anesthetic (ether), Veterinary Bottle (ketamine), Tranquilizer Dart. Restraints/blunt: Lead Pipe, Duct Tape, Nylon Rope, Heavy-Duty Zip Ties, Steel Cuffs, Steel Shackles, Full Body Harness.

Any tool/drug/gear — even lower-tier toys — can turn up.

The Dungeon & Holds

Your base. Start with one basic template (hole-in-the-wall / cinderblock), buy more dungeons over time across owned properties, and expand each one's capacity for more holds. Each captive occupies one hold.

Per-hold upgrade tracks (12)

🔒 Security · ⛓️ Restraints · 💡 Lights · 🧸 Toys · 🍱 Food quality · 🍽️ Feed automation · 🚽 Toilet (can → bucket → plumbing) · 🚰 Water supply · 🛏️ Bedding · 📺 Entertainment · 🎨 Decor · 🌡️ Climate. Plumbed toilet/water zeroes water decay; auto-feeder zeroes food decay.

Dungeon-level security facilities

Supplies & the Dungeon Pool

Supplies live in two tiers: each hold has its own reserve, and each dungeon has a shared POOL every hold draws from when its own reserve runs dry — no real stock cap, filtered evenly across holds.

  • Food / Water — a hungry/thirsty captive auto-consumes from her hold first, then the pool. She survives 5 game days without food, 3 without water (plumbing makes water automatic) before health drains.
  • Condoms — stock them so johns can glove up (see Johns). No stock = johns go raw.
  • Drugs — stock the pool and addicted captives self-dose to stave off withdrawal (they never self-OD; only you can OD her).

Stock the pool from the room's 🏪 Dungeon Pool panel (convert inventory items into shared units), or drop straight into a hold's reserve.

🚚 Auto-Logistics (upgrade)

Sick of hand-stocking every pool? Buy the Auto-Logistics upgrade and it auto-distributes supplies from your universal inventory into every dungeon's shared pool each tick — up to per-kind slider targets you set once in the Dungeons page. It pulls the best-value source first and tops every pool to your set level so you stop babysitting the stock. Set it and forget it; your meat stays fed, watered, gloved, and dosed on its own.

Drugs & Addiction

Drugs run on pharmacokinetic curves (onset → peak → wear-off) driving her high, speech, arousal, and pliability. Forced dosing makes her instantly more pliable (arousal up, escape-will down) and builds addiction.

Addiction & withdrawal

Dosing racks up per-drug addiction; cut her off and withdrawal hits her mood + stamina + escape-risk (never her health directly). Stock the dungeon drug pool and she'll self-dose to stay functional — she never self-ODs.

💰 Money, Vault & the Underworld

Your cash lives in two places: the 🗄️ Vault (everything you own, built into the finance tab) and your pocket (the cash you carry). Withdraw a wad from the vault to your pocket for free at home; in the field you choose how to pay at checkout — 💵 Pocket (what you carry), 🏦 run home to the Vault (pays from your stash but burns game-time on the round trip), or 💳 Charge it to a credit card.

💳 Credit cards

Spend money you don't have — but it's debt. The limit GROWS the more you charge and pay off, and the balance compounds interest (~3%/game-day) that spirals past the limit if you ignore it. Riskier than cash, woven through the whole economy.

🦈 Loan sharks

Three escalating lenders (Gutterbank / Knuckles / Syndicate) drop fast cash into your vault against a deadline. The vig compounds every tick, and an overdue loan bleeds WANTED heat — borrow shallow or it owns you.

⛓️ How to Best Keep Your Slaves

An ignored, terrified, unsecured fresh captive will work her escape-risk meter up and break for it. Containment decides caught vs gone. Keep her, in order of leverage:

  1. Care lowers the bar. Love/caress/heal/feed acts drop escape-risk + raise bond + indoctrinate. A few care acts on a fresh captive keeps her put.
  2. Bond her. High Stockholm captives stop trying — a bonded girl's risk can drift down on its own.
  3. Upgrade containment. Security + restraint tiers, a 🐕 guard dog, motion sensors, electrified perimeter, and an auto-sedation collar all shave the rise and make a maxed bar auto-contain. Once invested, escapes stop being something you manage by hand.
  4. Feed + water her. Stock her hold or the dungeon pool; plumbing makes water hands-off. Starvation/dehydration crater health.
  5. Don't over-use her. ~10 extreme acts spends her — let her recover, or spread the rough stuff across the roster.
  6. Manage withdrawal. Stock pool drugs so addicted girls self-dose; withdrawal spikes escape-risk.
  7. Sedate or nugget the flight risk. A tranquilizer dart buys time; a full nugget can never run.

The warning + the timer. When a captive's escape-risk climbs into the danger band you get a pop-up naming exactly WHY she wants out (her mood, bond-debt, a bleak hold, pain, neglect) with a one-click jump straight to her hold — go fix the cause. If the bar maxes she doesn't vanish instantly: a grace timer ticks down while she pries at the door — rush in, calm her with care acts, and the bolt is cancelled. Let it run out and she breaks for it.

If she does escape, recapture her before she ages out of the recovery window — a girl who gets away clean talks, and that's a big WANTED spike. Worse: if you're already MOST WANTED when she slips loose, she runs straight to a force that's already hunting you — the law closes in hard.

🚨 How to Stay Out of Jail

WANTED (0–100) is pure law-heat — distinct from BUZZ (good underground rep) and Notoriety (legacy stat). It builds from hunts, escapes, deaths, sloppy disposal, and corpse content; it decays slowly. High tiers roll per-tick raids.

What raises it

Botched/witnessed captures · escapes (worst when she gets away clean and talks) · captive deaths · sloppy disposal · selling corpse/necro content · letting a non-bonded captive walk · a body left to rot.

What cools it

Time (slow decay) · soundproofing + security facilities · WANTED-decay trophy perks · paying a raid fine · tenderness (care/feed/water acts quietly bleed a sliver of heat off) · the dirty-cop payroll (below).

🚨 Dirty-cop payroll — mid/end-game heat management

So a flush operation isn't getting stopped + jailed every single day, you can put the law itself on your books. At the top tier even MOST-WANTED raids become rare. Bought from the dashboard; stacks with the defenses below.
